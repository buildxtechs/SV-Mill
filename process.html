Our Milling Process

A 100% automated, hygienic journey from paddy to premium rice.

1. Cleaning & Pre-processing

Raw paddy is thoroughly cleaned to remove dust, stones, and impurities using advanced air and screen separators.

2. Parboiling & Drying

The paddy is soaked and steamed to retain nutritional value, then passed through automated dryers for uniform moisture content.

3. Milling & Polishing

The husk and bran layers are gently removed. The rice is then polished to achieve its signature silky texture and shine.

4. Optical Sorting (Sortex)

Our imported high-tech optical sorters scan every individual grain, rejecting discolored, broken, or defective rice instantly.

5. Hygienic Packaging

The 100% clean premium rice is automatically weighed and sealed in export-quality bags, completely untouched by human hands.
